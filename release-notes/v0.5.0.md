# Nocto 0.5.0: editor effects

The video editor gets a GPU effect system. Every effect runs as a D3D11 shader on the frames being edited, so the live preview and the export match. Exports go through your hardware encoder (NVENC, QSV or AMF).

## New
- **Effect stack per clip** (Inspector > Effects): Color Correction (exposure, contrast, saturation, temperature/tint, lift/gamma/gain), Curves, **.cube LUT import**, Gaussian Blur, Sharpen, Vignette, Film Grain, **Chroma Key** (green/blue with spill suppression) and Invert. Effects can be reordered, toggled and removed.
- **Keyframes on every numeric parameter**, with linear, ease in/out or hold interpolation. Click the diamond next to a parameter to add a keyframe, and right-click it for interpolation and previous/next keyframe. Keyframes appear on the selected clip in the timeline, where you can drag them.
- **Masks on any effect**: rectangle or ellipse, with feather, rotation and invert, all keyframable.
- **Transform & crop**: position, scale, rotation, opacity and crop on each side.
- **Transitions**: crossfade, dip to black, wipe, slide and zoom. Use "Add transition from previous clip", then choose the type.
- **Speed and speed ramps**: 0.1x–8x per clip, or keyframe Speed for a ramp. Audio either keeps its pitch (WSOLA time-stretch) or is muted.
- **Titles and shapes**: **+ Title** adds a text layer (any installed font, bold/italic, color, outline, shadow). **+ Shape** adds a rectangle or ellipse (corner radius, fill, outline). Both are animatable.
- **Picture-in-picture**: **+ Video track** / **+ Audio track** add tracks, and the x on a track header removes one. Higher video tracks draw on top.
- **Plugin effects**: shader-only (.json + .hlsl) and DLL video filter plugins show up under Add effect > Plugins, with keyframes and masks. In the editor, `TIME` is clip-local time, so exports are deterministic. See https://lukeroundy.github.io/nocto-releases/plugins.html#editor
- Effects, keyframes, speed, titles and tracks are saved in the project file (.gprproj). 0.4.x projects open unchanged.

## Fixes
- Project files now keep plugin video filters on clips (the plugin key was being dropped on save).
- Lossless smart cut is skipped automatically when a clip has effects, transforms, keyframes or speed changes.

## Internal
- New `Nocto.exe --test-effects [--ffmpeg <path>]`: renders every effect, mask, keyframe mode, speed mapping, transition, title and shape, and checks the pixels. It also checks that pitch is preserved in stretched audio (numerically, nothing is played), and exports a project with every feature and compares the decoded frames against the preview.
