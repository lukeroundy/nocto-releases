# Nocto 0.5.2: high-DPI and small-window layout fixes

- Works at every Windows display scale from 100% to 300%, and in small windows (down to 640x360 at 300%)
- Text, the logo and icons are rendered at the real pixel size, so they stay sharp at high DPI
- Moving Nocto to a monitor with a different scale resizes the UI right away
- Controls: the stats no longer slide under the Settings button
- Editor timeline: track names and buttons no longer overlap, ruler labels never run together, toolbar tools that don't fit move into a "..." menu, and the transport buttons wrap instead of getting cut off
- Settings, mixer, sources, inspector and multistream list: widgets fit their labels, long text wraps, and checkbox columns scale
- Panels keep their proportions when the window is resized
